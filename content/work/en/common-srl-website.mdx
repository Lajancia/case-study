## Context

*This case study reconstructs real work at the level of technique and outcome. Internal system names and non-public details are omitted.*

As an intern at a Milan-based startup, I owned the official website and the magazine platform — both the display side and the purchase flow — for a fashion-magazine and event-marketing service.

Two things were true when I started. The site was **static HTML**, so every new page meant copying markup around. And the **domain and server were managed outside the company**. The company could not directly touch either the code of its own website or the place that code ran.

The site is viewable at [common-mag.com](http://www.common-mag.com/).

## What I did

### 1. Rebuilt static HTML on Vue.js/Vuetify

I broke the repeated markup into **Vue.js** components and rebuilt the official website and the magazine display and purchase pages on top of the **Vuetify** component library. The mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints went in during the rebuild — far cheaper than retrofitting responsive behaviour onto static HTML later, when the component structure was being redrawn anyway.

### 2. Moved the domain and server in-house

With the domain and server managed externally, even a deploy had to go through a third party, and the company held no control over its own assets. I **hosted the server under the company's name and transferred the domain to the company**. Deploys and DNS changes end inside the company from then on.

It was not a typical scope for an intern, but rebuilding a frontend while the company still cannot control where it runs only gets you halfway, so it belonged in the same piece of work.

### 3. Improved the portfolio navigation

I reworked the navigation flow of the portfolio pages so that visitors arriving from social media could review the studio's work and follow through to a business inquiry.

### 4. Worked with a multinational team

I collaborated with the on-site Milan team, turning the fashion-magazine and event-marketing service's requirements into agreed screens and then into implementation.
