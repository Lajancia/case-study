## Context

*This case study reconstructs real work at the level of technique and outcome. Internal system names and non-public details are omitted; the measured figures are as recorded.*

As an intern at a Milan-based startup, I helped build the official website and the magazine platform — both the display side and the purchase flow — for a fashion-magazine and event-marketing service, working with a multinational team from screen planning through implementation.

The site I built is **still running** at [common-mag.com](http://www.common-mag.com/).

## What I did

### 1. Responsive implementation with Vue.js/Vuetify

I built the official website and the magazine display and purchase pages with **Vue.js** and the **Vuetify** component library, designing the responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop from the start rather than retrofitting it later.

### 2. Portfolio UX improvements

I improved the navigation flow of the portfolio pages so that visitors arriving from social media could review the studio's work and follow through to a business inquiry.

### 3. Working with a multinational team

I collaborated with the on-site Milan team to translate the fashion-magazine and event-marketing service's requirements into screens.

## Results

Built in Milan in 2022 as an intern, and viewable at [common-mag.com](http://www.common-mag.com/).

It was the first project where I owned the full frontend arc — screen planning through implementation and delivery. Translating the design into Vuetify's component system meant setting the mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints up front rather than retrofitting them, and turning requirements as they came up in a multinational team into agreed screens.
